  • 다른 사람을 찍을 때는 반드시 그 사람의 동의를 받아 주세요.
  • 삭제: 첫 화면 [⚙ 옵션] › 기타 › [🗑 내 데이터 지우기]로 저장된 사진과 기록을 모두 지울 수 있고, 앱을 삭제해도 함께 지워집니다.

Barber Premium — Privacy Policy

Effective: 29 October 2026 · App: Barber Premium (com.skjh1018.barberpremium)

1. Summary

  • No sign-up or login. We do not ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Camera and photos stay on your device. They are never uploaded or shared.
  • No ads and no advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • The app purchase is processed by Google Play. The app does not connect to the internet.

2. Camera and photos ("Photo customer")

  • The camera permission is requested only after you choose "Create a customer from a photo" and tap the consent button. If you deny it, everything else still works and you can pick a photo from the gallery instead.
  • The photo is cropped to the face area and saved in the app's private storage on your device. Skin tone, hair colour, hairline and hair length are estimated on the device only to build a 3D practice customer. No face recognition or biometric processing is used. Photos and results are never sent to a server or shared with anyone.
  • Please get permission from anyone you photograph.
  • To delete: tap [⚙ Options] › Other › [🗑 Delete my data] on the title screen, or uninstall the app.

3. Data stored on the device

Practice records, settings and progress (coins, level) are stored only on your device and can be deleted with [⚙ Options] › Other › [🗑 Delete my data] or by uninstalling.

4. Purchases

  • Payments are handled by Google Play under Google's Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We never receive card details or purchase records.
  • The app does not connect to the internet and sends no data to any server.

5. Voice narration

Narration uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. The app does not use the microphone.

6. Children

The app is made for barber and hairdresser practice and is not directed at children under 14. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

7. Deletion requests and contact

Device data can be deleted at any time with [⚙ Options] › Other › [🗑 Delete my data] or by uninstalling. For any question, email us. We will respond within 7 days.
